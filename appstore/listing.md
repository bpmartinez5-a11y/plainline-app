## Flagged for Ben (not in the copy)
- **The owner app doesn't exist yet.** Today the front desk is only the live demo (demo.html, chats live in one browser tab). The app needs real owner sign-in, each business's chats and bookings from Supabase, and push alerts before it can be submitted.
- **[APP BUILD]** Push alerts on hand-offs are the native feature that gets this past Apple's "just a website" rule. They're described only in the review notes, not the store copy, until they ship.
- **Screenshots** are the demo's front desk with made-up chats for the demo's fictional salon (appstore/seed.js), demo labels and the customer tab hidden, and "Sample business and customers shown" on every image. Apple wants screenshots of the actual app: retake them from the real build before submitting.
- **Deposits:** the copy says "take a deposit" as the site does. Make sure the app's payment flow uses an approved processor; Apple doesn't require in-app purchase for payments for real-world services, but the review notes should say deposits are for in-person services.
- Apple may treat a business app that only works for existing customers as fine (guideline 3.1.3(f)-style free companion apps), as long as the demo account shows it working.
