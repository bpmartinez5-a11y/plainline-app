Questions

What is Plainline?

Plainline is an AI agency. We bring AI into how your team works and build AI tools for it, and websites and apps are coming next. We also run the work behind your business: for one location, an AI front desk that answers customers by text and website chat and books them into open times; for a large company, a back-office team that Plainline staffs and runs.

Can you help our team use AI like Claude at work?

Yes. We research your industry and your company, pick the AI model that fits your work and your rules (Claude or another enterprise model), and set it up with your team's own tools, playbooks and skills. Then we train your people and keep it current.

Can someone run our back office for us?

Yes. On the Back Office plan, Plainline runs the work for you: answering customers, scheduling, following up on leads and quotes, keeping records current and reporting to your managers. AI handles the volume, Plainline's operators handle the judgment calls, and your managers can see the work. What we run is scoped in your walkthrough.

How do we stop missing customer messages after hours?

Plainline is set up to answer texts and website chats right away, day or night, from your own services, hours and policies. It tells customers it's an automated assistant. Anything it can't answer goes to a person, so customers aren't left waiting.

Who follows up on every lead and quote?

From the Front Office plan up, Plainline does. New inquiries and open quotes get a follow-up on a schedule you approve.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is quoted for your company after a walkthrough and our research on your business. We don't publish prices.

How fast do you reply?

We reply to every message within 24 hours, and often sooner. Book a walkthrough, and we answer you by email.

Can I step in on a conversation?

Yes. You can read every conversation and take over any of them. Plainline is also set up to hand off on its own when a customer needs a person.

Can I see it working first?

Yes. The live demo lets you text as a customer and watch the front desk side at the same time.
