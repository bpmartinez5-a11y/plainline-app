Privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Plainline is operated by Plainlineops LLC, a Kansas limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what Plainline collects on this website, why, who helps us handle it, and how you can have it deleted. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, email, business name, phone number, your business's website if you give it, your answers to five quick questions (your role, where the business is, team size, kind of business and how soon you want it running) and your message, only when you send the form. The walkthrough chat asks for the same details when you book. We use your answers to see whether a walkthrough fits before we reply, your website to learn about your business, and the rest to reply to you and to talk with you about a setup. We call your phone number only about your request, and we never text you without your OK.
  • Seller questions. If you answer the questions on our seller page, we save your name, email, your phone number if you give one, and your answers, the same way as the contact form. We use them only to reply to you about selling with Plainline.
  • Walkthrough chat. The chat on our home page is an AI assistant. What you type in it is sent through our server to our AI provider to write the next reply. If you share your website's address, our server reads that site's public home page and sends that text to our AI provider too. We don't save the chat on our server. Anthropic handles those messages under its own API terms and may keep them for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. If you book a walkthrough from the chat, we save your name, email, the messages you typed, the website address and a short summary the assistant wrote, the same way as the contact form, and we use them only to reply to you and to talk with you about a setup.
  • Live demo. Messages and details you type into the demo stay in your browser tab and are gone when you refresh or close it. If the demo's AI replies are turned on, the messages in that chat are sent through our server to our AI provider to write the next reply. We don't save them. Anthropic handles those messages under its own API terms and may keep them for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring; we don't control that, which is one more reason to use made-up details in the demo. If message sorting is turned on, your last few demo messages also go to TypeSafe AI, which only labels them (for example, a booking request or something urgent) so a person can be brought in; we don't save its answers. Settings you change on the demo's setup screen are saved only in your own browser.
  • Phone calls. When you call our business number, an automated AI assistant answers. Its first sentence says it is an automated assistant and that the call is recorded. ElevenLabs runs the assistant's voice: it turns what you say into text and the replies into speech, and an AI model (Claude, made by Anthropic) that ElevenLabs runs for us writes the replies. ElevenLabs keeps the call recording, its transcript and a short summary of the call for 90 days, then deletes them. If you ask for a walkthrough or for a person, we save your name, the number you called from and any number you asked us to use, your business name and the kind of work it does, your email if you give it, the time you picked, why you asked for a person, a short note and a call reference number, the same way as the contact form. When the assistant can't take your call, or no one picks up when it connects you, we save the number you called from, and you can leave a voicemail. Twilio records it and writes a text copy, which we add to your request. We delete the recording and Twilio's copy after 90 days. Each request goes by email or text to the person at Plainline who calls you back, and that person can play your voicemail. We use these only to reply to you and to talk with you about a setup. If you ask for a person during our hours (8 AM to 8 PM Central), we connect your call to someone at Plainline. We never call you with an AI voice.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our host receives your IP address and basic browser information when you visit. Our server also holds your IP address in memory, never on disk, for up to a day (a little longer if the server stays running), only to limit how many AI replies and form sends one visitor can make in the demo, the walkthrough chat, the contact form and the seller questions. When you call us, it holds your phone number the same way, only to limit how many calls one number can make in a day. We count page visits with Vercel's cookieless analytics, which shows us totals like visits per page and where visitors came from, never who you are.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising cookies unless you choose "Allow" (see Ad measurement), and our own page counts don't follow you across sites.
  • We don't knowingly collect information from children. This site is for businesses and adults.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as "No thanks" to ad measurement and don't ask. Browsers can also send a "Do Not Track" signal; there is no common standard for it, so we rely on your "Allow" or "No thanks" choice and on Global Privacy Control instead.
  • When we make data de-identified so it can't be tied to a person or business, we keep it that way: we don't try to re-identify it, and we require anyone we share it with to make the same promise.

Who helps us run the site

These companies help us run the site. Each handles data under its own terms, as described below:

  • Vercel hosts the website and the demo server, and counts page visits.
  • Supabase stores contact form messages, walkthrough requests from the chat and from phone calls, and the texts we send and receive.
  • Stripe sends our invoices and takes payments from clients. We never see or store full card or bank numbers.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that writes demo replies, walkthrough chat replies, automated text replies and the phone assistant's replies, when AI replies are turned on.
  • ElevenLabs runs the phone assistant: its voice, the speech-to-text and the AI model that answers. It keeps call recordings, transcripts and call summaries for 90 days.
  • TypeSafe AI sorts your latest demo messages (for example, a booking request or something urgent) so a person can be brought in, when message sorting is turned on.
  • Twilio sends and receives our text messages, carries our phone calls and keeps voicemails and their text copies until we delete them after 90 days.
  • Resend sends our team's work emails, such as the notice about your call or voicemail to the person who calls you back.
  • Google Workspace runs our email, so our replies to you go through it, and our walkthrough booking page (Google Calendar), where you pick a time.
  • Google Ads and Meta measure which of our ads bring visitors, only if you choose "Allow" (see Ad measurement).
  • Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to load them.

Ad measurement

We sometimes advertise on Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram). To learn which ads lead to a booked walkthrough, our pages can load Google's and Meta's measurement tags, but only after you choose "Allow" on the notice at the bottom of the page. Until then, and if you choose "No thanks", they don't load and set no cookies.

If you allow it, Google and Meta receive that you visited our page, the page address, your IP address and browser information, the ad you came from, and whether you tapped the link to book a walkthrough. They can use their cookies and what they know about your account under their own terms (Google, Meta). We never send them what you type into our forms, the chat or the demo. Sharing for ads this way can count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws; choosing "No thanks", or using Global Privacy Control, opts you out.

Text messages

We text you only if you text us first or ask us to, for example to confirm or move a walkthrough. Each text is about your request. When you text our business number, an automated assistant may answer, and its first reply says so. A person takes over when needed. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop our texts at any time. Reply HELP, or use the contact form, for help.

Replies to your texts can come at any hour. Texts we start, such as a walkthrough reminder, go out only between 10 AM and 7 PM Central time. Texting us is not a condition of buying anything.

We don't sell your mobile number, and we don't share it or your consent to get texts with anyone for their marketing. We keep a record of each text we send you and of how you agreed to get texts, so we can show we had your OK.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form messages and requests from phone calls for as long as we need them to reply to you and follow up about a setup, then delete them. Call recordings and transcripts are deleted after 90 days. You can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us. Send the request through the contact form and say what you'd like us to do. We'll confirm when it's done. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Security

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Contact form messages are stored so that the public website can add new messages but can't read them back.
