What Plainline does

Plainline brings AI into how small service businesses run.

  • An AI front desk answers missed calls and texts, day and night. It always says it is automated.
  • It books the job, or it sends the urgent ones to the owner in under 5 minutes.
  • It follows up on quotes and shows the owner which calls became jobs.
  • Plainline sets it all up. The owner installs nothing.

Who we help

  • Heating, cooling and plumbing shops
  • Auto repair, tire, body and detail shops
  • Golf courses, driving ranges, simulator venues and teaching pros

Owner-run, where the phone brings in the work and the owner is too busy to answer it.

We do not take financial services, gun shops or gun ranges.

What you do

Your one job is to book the walkthrough. You do not sell the product, quote or sign. Plainline gives you a short guide, the words to use and a one-page lead form.

How a lead moves

  1. Talk to an owner you know. Ask what happens to the calls they miss.
  2. If they name a problem, ask for 20 minutes with Plainline.
  3. Send Plainline the lead form the same day. The lead is yours for 90 days.
  4. Plainline runs the walkthrough, quotes and tells you the result.

The rules

  • Never say a price. Plainline quotes after the walkthrough.
  • Tell owners that Plainline pays you.
  • Talk as yourself. No auto-dialers, bought lists or mass texts.
  • Stop when someone says no.

How you get paid

You earn a share when a client you bring pays Plainline. The terms are in a written seller agreement that you sign before you start.
