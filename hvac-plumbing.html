Questions

What happens when I miss a call on a job?

The front desk texts the caller back within the hours you set, and it answers their texts right away. It asks what they need, books the job into an open time on your schedule, or sends the urgent ones to you in under 5 minutes. You see every call it caught in one list.

How is this different from an answering service?

A message service takes a note, and someone still has to call back. The front desk answers right away from your own services, hours and rules. It books the job when it can, hands the rest to you, and shows you which calls became booked jobs.

Is it a person or AI?

It is an AI front desk, and it always tells customers it is automated. Anything it can't answer, and anything urgent, goes to you or to the person you name.

What about emergencies like a burst pipe or no heat?

You tell us what counts as urgent during setup. Those calls go to you or your on-call tech in under 5 minutes. If a send fails, it goes again by a backup method, and every send is logged.

Do I have to install or learn anything?

No. Plainline sets it up and tests it with you. You do three things: a 20-minute walkthrough, name one person for urgent calls, and OK the words the front desk uses.

How do I stop missing calls in my HVAC or plumbing business?

Do three things. Text every missed caller back within the hours you set. Give after-hours messages one clear owner. Let customers book a time by text. The front desk does the first and the last for you, and it sends urgent jobs to the person you name in under 5 minutes.

What is missed-call text back?

It sends a text, within the hours you set, to a caller you could not answer. It works best when the text can also answer questions and book a time. The front desk does both, logs every call it caught, and always tells customers it is automated.

Should a small shop use an AI or a live answering service?

AI answers right away and does not get tired in a busy week. A person is better for an upset or unusual customer. The safe setup uses both: the front desk gives the first answer, and it hands the hard ones to you.

Can it book jobs by text?

Yes. During setup we give it your real open times. It offers those times by text and books the job. Anything it can't book goes to you.

Does Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan answer customers for me?

Those tools run your jobs, schedule and invoices. Ask their companies whether they answer texts for you. Plainline is a front desk, not job software.

Do I have to tell customers they are talking to an AI?

Plainline's front desk always tells customers it is automated. Rules differ by state, so ask your own lawyer about any other tools you use.
